Privacy

Short and simple: how this website handles your information.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no advertising trackers. We only get the information you choose to send us, for example when you email info@capeislandcafe.com or call 774-2-ISLAND. We use it only to reply to you, and we don't sell it.

Ordering online

The "Order Now" buttons take you to our online store, which is run by Square. Your order and payment details are handled by Square under Square's privacy notice (opens in a new tab).

"Ask Us" chat

The Ask Us helper runs in your browser and answers from our menu. We don't store your questions. If you use the microphone or read-aloud buttons, your browser's own speech features are used, and your browser maker's privacy terms apply.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Some pages show a Google Map. These services may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, when a page loads.

Questions

Email info@capeislandcafe.com with any privacy question.
