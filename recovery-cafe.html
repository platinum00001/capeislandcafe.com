About us

A place to belong, one cup at a time

Cape Island Café is a Recovery Café: a community space for anyone affected by addiction, mental health challenges, or simply the weight of everyday life. Here you can have a meal, catch up with friends, and find support, all without judgment.

We're home to Root to Rise, a program of Inspiration Is Everywhere, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We believe that recovery happens in community. Whether you're celebrating a milestone, taking a first step, or supporting someone you love, there's a seat for you here.

Everyone deserves a place where they're known, welcomed, and loved.The heart of the Recovery Café
What we offer

Coffee, community & connection

Coffee & food

Fresh coffee and wholesome, comforting food in a relaxed, sober-friendly setting.

Community

Recovery support

Talk with a Root to Rise recovery coach over coffee, and find encouragement and connection.

Open to all

No matter where you are on your journey, you're welcome here, just as you are.

Root to Rise

A meal and someone to talk to

If you're in recovery, you can get a free or low-cost meal here and sit down with a recovery coach. No pressure, no judgment, just a warm plate and a friendly ear.

Root to Rise is a program of Inspiration Is Everywhere, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Want to volunteer or support Root to Rise? Email roottorisema@gmail.com.
