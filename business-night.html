Local Business Celebration Night · 5–7 PM · Falmouth

Your Team Deserves a Night

Hit an anniversary? Won a Best Of? Crushed a goal, or have a team that’s earning glowing reviews? Bring everyone to Cape Island Café for an evening that’s all about you. Local businesses, nonprofits, first responders, town and city agencies, sports, school and community teams: here in Falmouth, we lift each other up.

Reserve the nightCall 774-2-ISLAND

A night to be recognized

Behind every local business, nonprofit, fire and police crew, town office and hometown team are people who show up every day for this community. Whatever the milestone (a company anniversary, an employee you want to honor, a Best Of win, a championship season, an internal goal reached, or simply a stack of great customer reviews), it deserves more than a quick post. Reserve a night, and we’ll roll out the welcome: your name on our screen, samples from our kitchen, and a room full of your people, from 5:00 to 7:00 PM.

And because good business gives back, 10% of the night’s proceeds go to the charity of your choice. Locals supporting locals, with a little extra for the community we all share.

What your night includes

We promote you

We spread the word about your organization and invite Falmouth to come celebrate with you.

Food samples

Select food samples from our kitchen for your guests. $20 suggested donation per person, in-house.

You’re on our screen

Your organization’s information is shown on our in-café screen all evening.

10% to your charity

10% of the night’s proceeds go to a charity of your business’s choice.

We celebrate you

We raise a toast to your organization and your charity, right here in the café.

Everyone’s welcome

Bring your team, your regulars, your biggest fans. Everyone’s welcome, 5:00–7:00 PM.

Reserve your date

Businesses, nonprofits, first responders, agencies and teams all welcome. Choose how you’d like to reserve, pick a date, and tell us what you’re celebrating. We’ll reply to confirm your night.

Donate $100 & reserve

Lock in your night with a $100 donation that goes to the charity. Pay securely through Square, then submit the form below so we have your date.

Free request
