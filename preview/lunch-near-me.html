Our neighborhood

A weekday lunch spot for the people who keep Falmouth running

Cape Island Café sits at 316 Gifford Street, in the Homeport office park where Gifford Street meets Jones Road. It's a busy little corner of Falmouth. The courthouse is down the street, Falmouth Hospital is a few minutes away, and doctors' offices, dentists and small businesses fill the buildings all around us.

We opened to give this neighborhood a good, honest lunch: big salads, warm soups, hearty wraps and bowls, made fresh and ready when you are. We're also proudly 100% alcohol-free, so everyone is welcome at our table, whether you have twenty minutes between patients, an hour between court sessions, or a long afternoon of errands.

Here's a little guide to where we are and who we're right near. If you've ever searched for "lunch near me" from one of these spots, we hope you'll stop in.

About 3–5 minutes by car

Lunch near Falmouth Hospital & the emergency room

Falmouth Hospital, at 100 Ter Heun Drive, is under a mile from our door. Nurses, doctors and staff on a break, and families keeping someone company in the emergency room, can call ahead at 774-247-5263 or order online (opens in a new tab) and have lunch ready for a quick pickup.

If you're looking for soups and salads near Falmouth Hospital, try a bowl of our Kale & Bean Soup or Roasted Tomato & Red Pepper soup with a Harbor Chop Salad. It's the kind of lunch that keeps you going through a long shift.

Just down the street

Lunch near Falmouth District Court & Town Hall

Falmouth District Court, at 161 Jones Road, is just down the street from us, a short walk or about a minute's drive. Attorneys, court staff, jurors and anyone with a morning at the courthouse can grab a wrap and a coffee without going far.

Falmouth Town Hall, at 59 Town Hall Square, is about five minutes away. Town staff and committees can order lunch for pickup, or ask us about catering for meetings and trainings.

About 5 minutes from downtown

Lunch near Main Street, Falmouth

Working or shopping on Main Street? We're a short drive from downtown Falmouth, about five minutes by car, so it's an easy trip for a sit-down lunch or a pickup order on your way back to the office. Lunch in Falmouth doesn't have to mean hunting for a seat downtown at noon.

Gifford Street & Jones Road

We're part of the Homeport neighborhood ourselves, so lunch near Homeport Plaza is about as close as it gets. If you work at, or have an appointment with, one of the offices around us, we're a few steps away. We're right near:

Waiting on a lab visit or a dental appointment? Order ahead and pick up lunch on your way out. And if you have an appointment at one of these offices, you're welcome to our community discount (below).

We list our neighbors only to help you find us. Cape Island Café isn't affiliated with these businesses.

Martha's Vineyard bound?

Lunch on the way to the ferry

Catching a boat to Martha's Vineyard? The Island Queen leaves from Falmouth Harbor at 75 Falmouth Heights Road, about five minutes from us. The Steamship Authority terminal in Woods Hole is about five miles away, usually around 15 minutes by car depending on traffic. Order online, pick up a wrap and a soup to go, and enjoy lunch on the ride over.

Just remember we serve lunch Monday through Friday, 11 AM to 3 PM. We're closed on weekends.

Visiting Falmouth

Staying nearby? Lunch near your hotel

If you're staying in Falmouth for work, a hospital visit or a vacation, we're close to several local hotels and inns:

  • The Coonamessett, 311 Gifford Street, right across the street
  • Iris Hotel Cape Cod (formerly the Holiday Inn), 291 Jones Road, about two minutes away
  • The Admiralty Inn & Suites, 51 Teaticket Highway, about five minutes away
  • Falmouth Inn, 824 Main Street, about five minutes away
  • Inn on the Square, 40 North Main Street, about five minutes away
  • Palmer House Inn, 81 Palmer Avenue, about five minutes away

Skip the hotel snack bar and come have a fresh, healthy lunch in Falmouth with us.

Teachers & staff

Lunch near Falmouth schools

Morse Pond School is just up Jones Road, and Mullen-Hall School and Lawrence School are only a few minutes away. Falmouth High School and Falmouth Academy are a short drive too. Teachers and school staff can order lunch for pickup, and we'd love to help with lunch for staff meetings, professional development days and end-of-year celebrations through our catering.

What we're known for

Salads, wraps & soups in Falmouth

Salads. The Harbor Chop, Caesar, Kale Crunch, Island Chef and our Cashew Chicken Salad Plate. A big, fresh salad is the heart of a healthy lunch in Falmouth.

Wraps. Chicken Bacon Black Bean, Cashew Chicken, Chicken Caesar, Ham & Cheese and our Chili Wrap. They're filling, easy to eat on the go, and our answer to anyone looking for a sandwich shop in Falmouth.

Soups. Cape Road Chili, Kale & Bean, and Roasted Tomato & Red Pepper. Soup in Falmouth on a cold Cape day, made the way we like it.

Bowls and build-your-own. The Salmon Bowl, Southwest Rice Bowl and Chili Bowl, or build your own lunch plate for $14.50.

Beyond lunch, we offer catering in Falmouth for offices and events, custom meal prep, and frozen ice cream cheesecakes.
