Lunch near you

Your neighborhood lunch spot on Gifford Street

We're in the Homeport office park at Gifford Street and Jones Road, just down the street from Falmouth District Court and a few minutes from Falmouth Hospital, Town Hall and Main Street. Grab a salad, wrap or soup between appointments, on a shift break, or on your way to the ferry.

Community discount: 10% off for first responders, seniors, people in recovery, local restaurant workers, local business owners, health and wellness workers, and anyone with an appointment at a nearby office or Homeport Plaza business. Just let us know when you order.
