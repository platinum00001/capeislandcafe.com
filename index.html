About us

A place to belong, one cup at a time

Cape Island Café is a Recovery Café: a community space for anyone affected by addiction, mental health challenges, or simply the weight of everyday life. Here you can have a meal, catch up with friends, and find support, all without judgment.

We're connected to Cape Island Recovery, and we believe that recovery happens in community. Whether you're celebrating a milestone, taking a first step, or supporting someone you love, there's a seat for you here.

Everyone deserves a place where they're known, welcomed, and loved.
